Sponsor a Cup

Your shop's name on a real Free Fire MAX tournament - "YourShop Cup" - run end to end by XARENA ESPORTS. Players always enter free. You only fund the prize.

How it works

Three simple steps

Step 1

You pick the prize

Any budget works - even a small cash prize, free meals, mobile accessories or recharge vouchers. You decide what the winners get.

Step 2

We run the cup

Registrations, squad checks, match rooms, live scoreboard, results and winner announcements - all on the XARENA platform, free for every player.

Step 3

Your brand everywhere

The cup carries your name on the tournament page, live scoreboard, winner posts and community announcements.

What you get

Your brand in the arena

Naming rights

The tournament is announced and remembered as your cup - for example "Sharma Cafe Cup - Season 1".

Logo placements

Your logo and link on the cup's tournament page and on the live scoreboard players check all match long.

Winner announcements

Winner posts and certificates carry your brand - shared by players on their own social media.

Community shoutouts

Announcements in XARENA World Chat and community posts before, during and after the cup.

XARENA is a real, running platform: Season 1 with 48 squad slots, qualifiers into a grand final, verified squads, live scoreboards and an owner-verified registration process. Matches run on Free Fire MAX, the version officially available in India.

Straight answers

Good to know

What does it cost to run the cup?

Nothing. XARENA runs the cup free - you only fund the prize you choose. There is no fee, no commission and no hidden charge.

Do players pay anything?

Never. Entry is free for every player. XARENA does not collect money from players in any form - no entry fee, no in-app purchase, no betting.

Who handles the prize money?

You do. The prize goes directly from you to the winners. XARENA never touches your prize money - we announce the winners and hand you their details.

Is this model legal?

Free entry plus a sponsor-funded prize is the standard model used by tournament organizers. There is no wagering, no entry fee and no gambling element. (This page is information, not legal advice.)
