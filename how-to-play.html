How to Play

Get started in 3 easy steps — earn XP and rewards

01

Register Squad

Create or join your squad on the Squads page. Invite friends and lock your team before battle.

02

Check-in

Check-in opens 1 hour before match time and closes 15 minutes after start - confirm your slot from the match center.

03

Play & Earn XP

Compete in matches, win to earn XP on the Play page, climb the leaderboard and unlock exclusive rewards. XP only - never cash.
