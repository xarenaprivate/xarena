How it works

  • Host or join - hosts pick mode, map, rank range and mic; anyone can join with one tap.
  • Auto-close - a lobby fills to its slots and closes; stale lobbies die after 12 hours.
  • Same fair-play rules - scrim bans carry into tournaments. Room IDs come from the host in game, not this page.
