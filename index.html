Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about XARENA and the Ignition Cup.

What is XARENA ESPORTS?

XARENA ESPORTS is a Free Fire esports organisation from India: free squad tournaments, daily scrims, tracked rankings and a player community. It is founded and owned by Shivhari Rajput.

Is XARENA free to play?

Yes. Every tournament is free to enter, forever. There are no cash prizes and no entry fees. Players earn AP and XP, which are cosmetic only.

When is the next XARENA tournament?

The XARENA Ignition Cup is on Saturday 24 October 2026 at 8:00 PM IST, played online in Free Fire. 48 squads play 3 qualifier lobbies (8:00, 8:45 and 9:30 PM IST) and the top 16 overall advance to the Grand Final at 10:15 PM IST.

How do I register my squad for the Ignition Cup?

Create a free XARENA account, add your Free Fire IGN, verify your phone with the Instagram or in-app code from the owner, then register your squad on the Tournaments page. Registration is free.

Where do I find the room ID and password on match day?

On the match page in XARENA. The room ID and password are revealed 15 minutes before match start and are visible only to registered squads. Never share them outside your squad.
