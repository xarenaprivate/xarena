Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about XARENA tournaments and getting started.

What is XARENA ESPORTS?

XARENA ESPORTS is a Free Fire esports organisation from India: free squad tournaments, daily scrims, tracked rankings and a player community. It is founded and owned by Shivhari Rajput.

Is XARENA free to play?

Yes. Every tournament is free to enter, with no entry fees. A named sponsor may fund a cash prize and pay winners directly; XARENA does not collect entry fees or hold prize money. AP and XP cannot be bought or redeemed for cash.

When is the next XARENA tournament?

Season 2 schedule and registration are on the Tournaments page. Check the Tournaments page for current event details.

How do I find tournament registration details?

Open the Tournaments page to check each event's current status and registration details.

Where do I find the room ID and password on match day?

On the match page in XARENA. The room ID and password are revealed 15 minutes before match start and are visible only to registered squads. Never share them outside your squad.
