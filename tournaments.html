Format & Rules

  • Mode: Squad Battle Royale (4 players per squad, up to 2 substitutes).
  • Structure: 48 squads split into 3 qualifier groups. Top 16 overall advance to the Grand Finals.
  • Points: placement points + 1 point per kill. Full points table published before match day.
  • Fair play: no hacks, no teaming, no emulators unless the lobby is marked emulator-allowed. Violations = instant disqualification.
  • Schedule & prize pool: revealed on this page as soon as registrations close — announced by the management, never guessed.
